The Gypsy Swing Revue Band

The Band

Gypsy Swing Revue is a group based in the Denver metro area of Colorado. They perform music in the "Hot Club" style popularized by Django Reinhardt and Stephane Grappelli in Paris during the 30's and 40's. The focus is on tight arrangements with plenty of room for jazz improvisation. With a mix of instrumental pieces and vocals, this energetic music is fun for everyone.

{% include 'core/partials/viewer.html' with image_prefixes=image_prefixes id="band-viewer" %}

Formed in the before times, by Elliot Reed, they have been a staple of the Denver music scene. Their acclaimed show "Under Paris Skies ft. Lannie Garret" sold out shows in the Clocktower Cabaret. They have performed live on KUVO radio, and at many Colorado music festivals. Their exceptionally long stint at The Irish Snug was dubbed the best place to go in Denver on Wednesdays by The Denver Post. Newly reformed after a Covid hiatus, they are still swingin'.

The Musicians

The core of Gypsy Swing Revue consists of Elliot Reed, Michael Isabell, Eric Fellenstein, Robert Kerly, and Jean-Luc Davis.

{% include "core/partials/picture-musician.html" with name="elliot" description="" %}

Elliot Reed is a guitarist, vocalist, composer, arranger, producer, and educator. Elliot holds degrees from Casper College, and the University of New Mexico, and has been a member of the Pardise Valley Community College (Phoenix, AZ) faculty band.

When studying jazz, he thought the music of Django Reinhardt would be a minor stop, instead it turned into a lifelong obbsession! With a deep love of the manouche style of music, he has been integral to the gypsy jazz music scene in Colorado.

{% include "core/partials/picture-musician.html" with name="michael-isabell" description="" %}

Colorado native Michael Isabell is a guitarist, educator, and dedicated advocate for the traditions of gypsy jazz and swing. Recognized for his strong rhythmic foundation, and engaging musical presence, he has spent many years studying, performing, teaching and sharing the music inspired by Django Reinhardt and beyond.

Michael's work is guided by a simple belief: great music is not just something we play, but something we share.

{% include "core/partials/picture-musician.html" with name="jean-luc" description="" %}

A Colorado native, Jean-Luc Davis began playing bass in high school and was a student in the inaugural session of the Colorado Conservatory for the Jazz Arts (CCJA) where he studied with Paul Romaine, Greg Gisbert, John Gunther, and Eric Gunnison. In recent years he has become a regular teacher for CCJA. Alongside trumpet player, Greg Gisbert, he leads the Tuesday night Jam Session at the Jazz Club Dazzle in Denver. Jean-Luc studied classical bass performance at the University of Nebraska Lincoln. When not on tour Jean-Luc Davis lives in Denver and works with a handful of private students.
