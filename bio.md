## Michael Isabell

Colorado native Michael Isabell is a guitarist, educator, and dedicated advocate for the traditions of gypsy jazz and swing. Recognized for his strong rhythmic foundation, and engaging musical presence, he has spent many years studying, performing, teaching and sharing the music inspired by Django Reinhardt and beyond.

A sought-after instructor and coach, Michael works with students across the United States and internationally through his private studio and GypsyAndJazz.com. His teaching emphasizes rhythm, musical fluency, creativity, and the deeper process of learning itself.

Beyond the classroom and stage, Michael is committed to building musical community. He hosts the long-running Hot Club of Olde Town jam session and founded the Rocky Mountain Swing Guitar Camp, bringing together musicians from diverse backgrounds to study, collaborate, and celebrate the enduring spirit of swing music and culture.

Whether performing, teaching, or facilitating workshops and events, Michael's work is guided by a simple belief: great music is not just something we play, but something we share.

## Jean-Luc Davis

Jean-Luc Davis is an active performer in Colorado and national music scene. In addition to leading his own ensemble, Jean-Luc plays with Gypsy Swing Revue, Tom Gershwin Quintet, Adam Bodine Trio, the Railsplitters, Jeff Austin Band,  Andy Hackbarth Band, Jesse Manley Band, Jayme Stone, the John Kite Quartet, and regularly accompanies Wonderbound. Additionally, Jean-Luc has performed with Ron Miles, Shane Endsley, Peter Sommer, 9th and Lincoln Orchestra, Art Lande, Greg Harris, the Aakash Mittal Quartet, Carmen Sandim Sextet, the Matt Fuller Group. 

 A Colorado native, Jean-Luc Davis began playing bass in high school and was a student in the inaugural session of the Colorado Conservatory for the Jazz Arts (CCJA) where he studied with Paul Romaine, Greg Gisbert, John Gunther, and Eric Gunnison. In recent years he has become a regular teacher for CCJA. Alongside trumpet player, Greg Gisbert, he leads the Tuesday night Jam Session at the Jazz Club Dazzle in Denver. Jean-Luc studied classical bass performance at the University of Nebraska Lincoln. When not on tour Jean-Luc Davis lives in Denver and works with a handful of private students.
